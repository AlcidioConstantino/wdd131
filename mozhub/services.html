Before you hire

  • Explain the task and the location as clearly as you can.
  • Ask what is included in an estimate and whether materials cost extra.
  • Agree on timing, payment expectations, and how changes will be handled.
  • For regulated or safety-critical work, ask about relevant training and qualifications.
