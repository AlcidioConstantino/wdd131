Professional Services in Mozambique

1. Site Name

MozHub

The name MozHub combines "Moz", referring to Mozambique, with "Hub", meaning a central place where people can find information and connect with professional services.

I chose this name because the website focuses on professional services in Mozambique. It is short, simple, and connected to the country and the purpose of the website.

Possible domain: mozhub.co.mz

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of MozHub is to provide information about professional services in Mozambique.

The website will introduce different types of services, including electricians, plumbers, mechanics, private tutors, photographers, and cleaners.

Each service category will provide information about what professionals in that area can offer. The website will help visitors understand the different services available and make it easier to find useful information.

3. Scenarios

These scenarios represent questions that visitors may have when using the website:

  • What professional services can I find on MozHub?
  • What services does an electrician provide?
  • What services can a plumber or mechanic offer?
  • What can a private tutor help me with?

4. Color Scheme

The MozHub color scheme uses professional and simple colors that create a clear and modern appearance. These colors will also be used in the website project.

Navy

Hex: #123554

Used for the header, main headings, navigation, and important interface elements.

Dark Blue

Hex: #0B2743

Used for borders, secondary elements, and areas that need stronger contrast.

Yellow

Hex: #F4D35E

Used for highlights, accents, buttons, and important visual elements.

White

Hex: #FFFFFF

Used for backgrounds and text displayed on dark-colored areas.

5. Typography

MozHub will use two primary fonts. Poppins will be used for headings and important interface elements, while Open Sans will be used for general text.

Poppins

Use: Main headings, section headings, navigation, buttons, and important titles.
